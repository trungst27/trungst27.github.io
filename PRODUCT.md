# Teung’s Space

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Teung curates the music library. Visitors browse and listen without signing in. Only an authorized administrator uploads or publishes music.

## Product Purpose

A personal website with a calm listening space, live Discord presence, and a future learning portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript and GitHub Pages frontend; Supabase for Auth, database and Storage.
- Local audio and official SoundCloud embeds share search, mood filters, favorites, queue, shuffle, repeat and default 50% volume.
- Admin supports drafts, preview, publishing, editing, ordering and hiding tracks.
- No Supabase project is configured yet. The owner asked for the interface first and setup instructions.
- Current scope: one Home with a compact corner music player and expandable collection, plus Admin. Focus timer and portfolio are later work.
- Read files before editing and keep local preview available. Commit, push and deployment need a separate instruction.

## Brand Commitments

Nickname Teung. Panda at the main profile; original photo and decoration in Discord. Preserve supplied personal information, social links, anime video and eight AI logos. The owner clarified the layout: restore the open After Hours Home and keep a compact glass now-playing widget at the bottom right, around one quarter of desktop width. The collection opens on demand above it. No separate Music tab or full-width bottom player.

## Evidence on Hand

Two MP3 files in assets/music and two owner-supplied SoundCloud links. Unconfirmed track metadata and mood tags remain empty. Do not invent personal information.

## Product Principles

- No horizontal scrolling.
- One audible source at a time.
- Separate drafts from published music.
- Explain missing cloud configuration; bundled music remains playable.
